# Mary Anne Minute — Series Bible

The complete production recipe for the Mary Anne Minute, so a new episode made tomorrow — by the original producer or anyone else — matches the sixteen that exist. This document stands alone; it describes the finished format, not any one workplace.

## What the series is

- Short narrated teaching videos, one beginner-level AI tip per episode, roughly 30–75 seconds each.
- The teaching content traces to Microsoft's own published Copilot guidance (Microsoft Learn and Microsoft Support). Nothing is invented tips-from-a-blog; each episode's source is recorded in its production script.
- Written for people who are new to AI assistants and a little wary of them: plain words, one idea, no jargon without an explanation.

## The standalone rule (applies to every episode, no exceptions)

Every episode stands completely alone. Any viewer may see any single episode without the others, so:

- No references to the series arc in narration or post copy: no "last time…", no "we end…", no "every minute so far…", no episode counts, no finale framing.
- Each narration opens on its own tip and closes on its own point.

## Naming

- The series name is **Mary Anne Minute** (Mary [space] Anne; capital M in Minute).
- The name appears **only in the post lead-in text** that introduces an episode (every post opens "Mary Anne Minute — <episode title>").
- The name **never appears inside a video**: no title card, no watermark, no spoken mention.

## No branding in the videos — colors only

- No logos, no company names, no watermarks, nothing employer-specific anywhere in a video.
- Branding is carried by color alone, using the palette below. This is deliberate: it's what makes the videos reusable in any setting.

## Palette

Primary palette (carries the main color impact of every layout):

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Globe Blue | `#00358E` |
| Primary | Blueprint Blue | `#0062FF` |
| Primary | White | `#FFFFFF` |

## Typography

- **Roboto** throughout. Roboto Regular for headlines; Roboto Medium or Light for body text.

## Voice

- **Aria** — feminine, American, friendly and warm (TTS voice id: `avocado_v2:MAI_01`). The same voice narrates every episode.
- Pace is natural conversational speed; narration teaches, it does not read the whiteboard aloud.

## Design pattern

- Globe Blue background, white type.
- The episode's example prompts appear as **white text on Blueprint Blue cards**, styled like typed prompts.
- One Gold accent per video, reserved for the single most important line.
- The presenter's whiteboard (see below) carries the tip's key points, appearing in sync with the narration, filled **top to bottom in narration order**.

## The presenter

- An illustrated presenter — a polished, semi-realistic corporate illustration of Mary Anne herself; not photorealistic, not a funny cartoon.
- Appearance, locked: chin-length light blonde bob with a side part; blonde clearly dominant with fine silver-white strands blended evenly throughout, slightly lighter at the front hairline; **never colored, never gray overall, no gray root patch**. Generators drift toward "gray" — always anchor to reference photos.
- Navy blue glasses frames.
- Setting: an analyst's desk; monitors show charts only (bar chart, pie chart) — never spreadsheet or text screens.
- A whiteboard beside her holds the episode's teaching points, which appear as the narration reaches them. Whiteboard text is **composited, not AI-generated**, so spelling is always exact.

## Tone

- A slight bit of humor and lightness — warm and a little wry, never stiff, never a comedy bit.
- The lightness goes in **both** places: the narration and the post lead-in text.
- Conversational teaching throughout: talk to the viewer the way you'd talk to a colleague.

## Episode package

Every finished episode consists of:

1. The video (MP4, 1920×1080).
2. The narration script.
3. The post copy, opening with the "Mary Anne Minute — <title>" lead-in.

Episodes are numbered in production order; numbering is bookkeeping only and is never referenced in any episode's content (see the standalone rule).
