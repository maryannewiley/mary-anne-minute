Mary Anne Minute — One Job at a Time

If a Copilot answer reads like it was written by three people in a hurry, count how many jobs were in the prompt.

Microsoft's guidance: no conflicting instructions, and no changing topics in the middle of a thread. Ask for the summary first. Read it. Then the announcement, then the outline, one job at a time.

And when the topic truly changes, open a fresh chat. Copilot does its best work on a clean desk.
