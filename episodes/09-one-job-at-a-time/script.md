# Episode 9 — One Job at a Time

## Narration script (Aria)

If a Copilot answer ever reads like it was written by three people in a hurry, check whether you asked for three things at once. Microsoft's guidance is plain: do not hand it conflicting instructions, and do not change topics in the middle of a thread. One job at a time. Instead of "summarize this report, draft the announcement, and make a slide outline," start with "summarize this report." Read it. Then ask for the announcement, built on that summary. Then the outline. Each step gets your full request and its full attention. When I catch myself typing "and also" for the third time, that is my cue to stop and split the ask. And when the topic truly changes, open a fresh chat. A new conversation resets the table. Copilot does its best work on a clean desk, one job at a time.
