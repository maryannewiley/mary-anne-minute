Mary Anne Minute — Talk to It Like a Colleague

If you talk to Copilot like a search engine, three keywords and a prayer, this one is for you.

Microsoft's advice is to keep it conversational. Write the way you would ask a colleague: full sentences, plain words, a little background. "Draft Thursday's agenda like last week's, with thirty minutes on the budget and fifteen on staffing."

That reads like a request, not a ransom note, and it gets a better answer. Copilot cannot hear your tone of voice. It reads your words just fine.
