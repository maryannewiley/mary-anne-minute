Mary Anne Minute — Don't Accept the First Answer

Your first Copilot answer is a draft, not a deliverable. (A few of mine have read like they were written by a very confident intern.)

Don't accept the first answer. Push on it:
- "Make it shorter."
- "Make it more conversational."
- "Put the action items in a table."
- "Rewrite this for leadership."
- "Show me links to the sources you used."

That last one matters. Asking for references is how you check whether the answer deserves your trust before it goes anywhere. Five extra prompts take about a minute, and they're the difference between AI output and your work product.
