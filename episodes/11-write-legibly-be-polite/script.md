# Episode 11 — Write Legibly. Be Polite.

## Narration script (Aria)

This one sounds like advice from your third grade teacher, and Microsoft puts it in writing anyway: write legibly, and be polite. Legibly means real sentences, with punctuation and capital letters, not a stream of shorthand Copilot has to decode. Polite means please and thank you, the way you would type to a person. Their guidance says kind, clear language gets better collaboration, and honestly, it costs you nothing. A prompt like "Please summarize this week's status report in three bullet points. Thank you" is not just nicer. It is clearer about the task, the format, and the length. I will admit I rolled my eyes at this one. Then I noticed my sloppiest prompts, the ones typed one-handed with a sandwich in the other, were the ones that came back wrong. Write it like you mean it. Copilot reads it that way.
