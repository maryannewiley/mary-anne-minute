Mary Anne Minute — Write Legibly. Be Polite.

This tip sounds like it came from your third grade teacher. Microsoft put it in writing anyway.

Write legibly: real sentences, punctuation, capital letters, not one-handed shorthand. And be polite. "Please summarize this week's status report in three bullet points. Thank you" is not just nicer, it is clearer about the job.

Costs you nothing, and it works. My sloppiest prompts are the ones that come back wrong. There may be a lesson in that.
