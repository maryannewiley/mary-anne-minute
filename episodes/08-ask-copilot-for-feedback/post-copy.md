Mary Anne Minute — Ask Copilot for Feedback

Here is a prompt most people never think to type: ask Copilot to push back on you.

Microsoft lists "ask for feedback" as a core prompting habit. Before a big request, try "What questions do you have for me before you start?" After a draft, ask "What would make this stronger?"

It will find the deadline you forgot and the date you contradicted yourself on. Mildly annoying, extremely useful. Let it ask the questions.
