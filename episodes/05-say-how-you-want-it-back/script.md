# Episode 5 — Say How You Want It Back

## Narration script (Aria)

Here is the step almost everybody skips, and it is the cheapest one. Tell Copilot how you want the answer back. Who is it for? What tone? How long? Bullets, a table, or a paragraph? Microsoft calls this the expectations part of the prompt, and it is the difference between a draft and a deliverable. Their example is a thank-you note: warm, casual, under eighty words, and the answer lands ready to send. At work, the same move sounds like this: "Draft a short email to the team announcing the new meeting time. Friendly, under one hundred words, with the three action items in bullets." Without those instructions, I get a page of formal prose and an editing job. With them, I get something I can send after one read. The answer was always going to be something. You might as well choose what.
