Mary Anne Minute — Say How You Want It Back

The cheapest step in prompting is the one almost everybody skips: saying how you want the answer back.

Name the audience, the tone, the length, and the format before you hit enter. Microsoft's own example is a thank-you note, "warm, casual, under eighty words," and the answer lands ready to send.

At work, it is the difference between a page of formal prose you have to edit and a draft you can send after one read. The answer was always going to be something. You might as well choose what.
