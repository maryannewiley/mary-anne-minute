# Episode 14 — Don't Start from Blank

## Narration script (Aria)

The blank prompt box has ended more good intentions than any meeting ever called. Good news: you often do not have to write the prompt at all. Microsoft built the Copilot Prompt Gallery, a library of prompts that already work, organized by job and task. Open it, find one close to what you need, a status update, a meeting follow-up, a summary, and run it as written, or adjust a word or two and make it yours. You can also save prompts that work for you and share them with your team, so nobody has to reinvent the weekly report prompt ever again. I keep my best three saved like recipes, because that is what they are. Somebody in that gallery has already solved the prompt you are staring at. Go borrow their homework. It is allowed here.
