Mary Anne Minute — The Four Parts of a Good Prompt

Ever get an answer from Copilot that is technically fine and somehow useless? Nine times out of ten, the prompt left something out.

Microsoft teaches that a good prompt has up to four parts: a goal, context, a source, and expectations. Only the goal is required. Put together, it sounds like this: "Using the attached status report, summarize our progress for leadership, with milestones, risks, and next steps in five bullet points."

Same tool, same minute of typing, completely different answer. Copilot cannot read your mind, no matter how clearly you think at it. Give it the four parts and it gives you work back.
