Mary Anne Minute — Get Your Inbox to Brief You

Your inbox knows what your week looks like. It just will not summarize itself. Ask Copilot, and it will.

Microsoft's example prompt: "What should be on my radar from emails last week?" Follow it with "Flag anything urgent and anything waiting on a reply from me," and Monday starts with a briefing instead of a dig.

Fair warning: the first list may be longer than you hoped. That is not Copilot exaggerating. That is your inbox, telling the truth for once.
