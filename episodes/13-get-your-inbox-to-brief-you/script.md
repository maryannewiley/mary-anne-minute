# Episode 13 — Get Your Inbox to Brief You

## Narration script (Aria)

Your inbox knows what your week looks like. It just refuses to summarize itself. Actually, it will, if you ask Copilot. One of Microsoft's own example prompts is, "What should be on my radar from emails last week?" Ask on a Monday morning and you get the threads that matter, the requests waiting on you, and the deadlines people buried in paragraph four. I have started doing this before I open a single message: "Summarize the emails from last week. Flag anything urgent and anything waiting on a reply from me." The briefing takes a minute, and my first hour stops being archaeology. Fair warning: the first time you try it, the list may be longer than you hoped. That is not Copilot exaggerating. That is your inbox, telling the truth for once.
