Mary Anne Minute — Break It Into Steps

Some jobs are too big for one prompt, and cramming them in is how answers come back mushy.

Break the work into steps and chain them: "Summarize these meeting notes." Then "Turn that into an outline." Then "Draft the team update from the outline." Each answer becomes the next prompt's starting point.

You steer at every step instead of hoping one giant request lands. Big job? Shrink the first step until it is easy to say yes to.
