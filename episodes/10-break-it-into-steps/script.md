# Episode 10 — Break It Into Steps

## Narration script (Aria)

Some jobs are too big for one prompt, and forcing them into one is why the answer comes back mushy. Microsoft teaches prompting as an iterative process, so break the work into steps and chain them. Start with the raw material: "Summarize these meeting notes." Read what comes back. Then, "Turn that summary into an outline with three sections." Then, "Draft the team update from that outline, friendly tone." Each answer becomes the starting point for the next prompt, and you get to steer at every turn instead of hoping one giant request lands. I built a whole quarterly report this way last month, one honest step at a time, and the only hard part was remembering I used to do it the hard way. Big job? Shrink the first step until it is easy to say yes to.
