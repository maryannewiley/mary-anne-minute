# Episode 16 — Check It Before You Send It

## Narration script (Aria)

Every piece of Copilot work should end the same way: with you, reading it before it goes anywhere. Microsoft is direct about this. AI content can be wrong, so check the answer for accuracy, and open the sources it used before you share it. Names, numbers, and dates deserve a second look, because those are exactly what a confident answer can get wrong. Read it like a stranger would. Does it say what you meant? Would you sign your name to it? I give every Copilot draft the same thirty seconds I would give my own writing, which is to say, the thirty seconds that catch the embarrassing part. Copilot is fast, tireless, and occasionally wrong with great confidence. You are the quality department. Nothing leaves with your name on it until it has passed your desk.
