Mary Anne Minute — Check It Before You Send It

Getting a good answer is only half the job. This one is about the thirty seconds after.

Microsoft is direct: AI content can be wrong, so check the answer before you share it, and open the sources it used. Names, numbers, and dates get a second look, because those are what a confident answer gets wrong.

Read it like a stranger. Would you sign your name to it? Copilot is the fast first draft. You are the quality department.
