# Episode 6 — Show It an Example

## Narration script (Aria)

Explaining the style you want is hard. Showing it takes ten seconds. One of Microsoft's standing rules for prompting is: give examples. Copilot follows a sample better than a description, so paste one in and say, "Rewrite this announcement in the style of the sample below." It will match the length, the tone, even the way the sample uses headings. I keep this one in my pocket for reports. Our team has a monthly summary format that leadership likes, so instead of describing it every time, I paste last month's summary and say, "Follow this format with this month's numbers." The first draft comes back wearing the right clothes. One caution: use a sample you would be proud to copy, because you will get more of exactly that. Choose your sample like it matters. It does.
