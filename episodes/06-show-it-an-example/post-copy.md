Mary Anne Minute — Show It an Example

Describing the style you want is hard. Showing it takes ten seconds.

One of Microsoft's standing rules for prompting is: give examples. Paste a sample and say, "Rewrite this announcement in the style of the sample below." Copilot will match the tone, the length, even the headings.

One caution: it will copy your sample faithfully, so pick one you would be proud to repeat. Choose your sample like it matters. It does.
