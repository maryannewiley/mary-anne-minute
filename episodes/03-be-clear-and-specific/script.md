# Episode 3 — Be Clear and Specific

## Narration script (Aria)

If your Copilot answers feel like they were written for somebody else, check the prompt first. Vague in, vague out. Microsoft's first rule of prompting is simple: be clear and specific. Name the topic. Say what it's for. Set the length. Pick the tone. Compare "write something about the training" with "write a short paragraph inviting new team members to Friday's training session, friendly tone, under one hundred words." The second one takes ten extra seconds and saves you a rewrite. I use it on my own writing too. When a paragraph of mine rambles, I paste it in and ask, "Can you help me rewrite this paragraph to make it clearer and more concise?" It trims the fat better than I do, mostly because it is not attached to my sentences. Specific is not extra work. It is the work.
