Mary Anne Minute — Be Clear and Specific

Vague in, vague out. If your Copilot answers read like they were written for somebody else, the prompt is the first suspect.

Microsoft's first rule of prompting: be clear and specific. Name the topic, say what it is for, set the length, pick the tone. It even works on your own writing: "Can you help me rewrite this paragraph to make it clearer and more concise?"

Ten extra seconds of specific saves you a full rewrite. Specific is not extra work. It is the work.
