# Episode 12 — Catch Up in One Prompt

## Narration script (Aria)

You know that feeling — out of one meeting and straight into three more? Copilot can hand you the thread back. One of the most useful prompts in Microsoft's own examples is also the shortest: "Recap /meeting." Type the slash, pick the meeting, and you get the discussion, the decisions, and the action items in one place. It works for the meeting you attended while answering email, and it is a genuine kindness for the one you missed entirely. I use it after our weekly project call: "Recap the meeting. What was decided, and what am I on the hook for?" Thirty seconds later I have my marching orders, including the one I apparently agreed to while muted. Catching up used to mean asking a coworker and getting the highlights reel. Now the first stop is one prompt, and the coworker gets better questions.
