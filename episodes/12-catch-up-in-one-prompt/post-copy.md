Mary Anne Minute — Catch Up in One Prompt

Missed a meeting, or attended one while answering email? We have all done both.

One of Microsoft's shortest example prompts does the catching up: "Recap /meeting." Type the slash, pick the meeting, and you get the decisions, the discussion, and the action items, including what you are on the hook for.

Thirty seconds, and you have your marching orders. The coworker recap can be the second stop now, not the first.
