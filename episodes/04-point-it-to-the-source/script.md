# Episode 4 — Point It to the Source

## Narration script (Aria)

Copilot guesses. That is not an insult, it is the design. If you do not tell it where to look, it will happily build an answer out of whatever it can find, and "close enough" can look very convincing. So point it. Microsoft's guidance is to name your source right in the prompt. Type a slash and reference the meeting: "Recap /meeting." Or name the material: "Write a summary based on all emails from Sam in the past two weeks." Now it is working from your documents and your conversations instead of the general fog. I do this before every status write-up. I point Copilot at the actual report and the actual meeting notes, and the summary comes back with the right names, the right dates, and no surprises invented for flavor. Give it a source, and it gives you your work back.
