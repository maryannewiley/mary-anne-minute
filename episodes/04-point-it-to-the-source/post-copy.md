Mary Anne Minute — Point It to the Source

Left to itself, Copilot will build an answer out of whatever it can find, and "close enough" can look very convincing.

So point it at the source. Type a slash and reference the meeting, "Recap /meeting," or name the material outright: "Write a summary based on all emails from Sam in the past two weeks."

Now it works from your documents instead of the general fog. Right names, right dates, and no surprises invented for flavor.
